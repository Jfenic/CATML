# Trademark Policy — CATML

This policy outlines the guidelines for using the CATML name, logos, and related trademarks.

---

## The CATML Name & Marks

"CATML" stands for **Controlled Agentic Toolkit for Machine Learning**.

The CATML wordmark, acronym, logo, and brand assets represent the quality, security, and integrity of the project. While the source code of CATML is licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**, the Apache license does not grant trademark rights.

---

## Permitted Uses

You may use the CATML mark without prior written permission in the following cases:

1. **Truthful Identification:** Referencing the official open-source software project (e.g., *"This application is built using CATML"* or *"Compatible with CATML v0.8"*).
2. **Community & Extensions:** Naming third-party plugins, connectors, or tools using descriptive phrasing, such as:
   - :white_check_mark: *"Awesome Dataset Plugin for CATML"*
   - :white_check_mark: *"CATML Connector for PostgreSQL"*
3. **Academic & Educational Work:** Referring to CATML in research papers, benchmarks, tutorials, and documentation.

---

## Prohibited Uses

Without prior written permission from the project maintainers, you may **not**:

1. Use "CATML" as the primary name or brand of a commercial software product or SaaS offering (e.g., naming a proprietary product *"CATML Enterprise"* or *"CATML Cloud"*).
2. Use CATML logos or trademarks in a way that suggests endorsement, sponsorship, or official affiliation where none exists.
3. Register domain names, social media handles, or trademarks containing "CATML" or confusingly similar variants.
4. Alter or distort official CATML brand marks or logos in marketing materials.

---

## Questions & Contact

If you have questions regarding this policy or wish to request permission for a specific use case, please reach out via the official repository issues or maintainer contact channels.
